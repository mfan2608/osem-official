About

Uniting generations of Semesrans

OSEM was founded to keep the bonds formed in the corridors, dorms and fields of SM Sains Rembau alive long after graduation. Today we connect alumni across industries and continents, while helping the next generation of Semesrans reach further than we did.

Whether you left last year or decades ago, there is a place for you here.
