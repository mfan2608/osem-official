A family that began
in the hills of Rembau

OSEM, the Old Semesrans' Association, is the official alumni body of Sekolah Menengah Sains Rembau. We exist to keep Semesrans connected to each other and to the school that shaped us.

Our Story

From classmates to a lifelong community

Every Semesran remembers the early-morning assemblies, the late-night prep sessions in the dorms and the friendships forged far from home. When those students graduated and scattered across universities, careers and countries, many wanted a way to stay together and to give something back to the school.

In 2012, a group of alumni formalised that wish by founding the Old Semesrans' Association. What began as a handful of batch representatives organising reunions has grown into a network spanning every graduating batch, with members in medicine, engineering, academia, public service, business and the arts.

“We are not only graduates of the same school. We are keepers of the same promise: to lift the next Semesran higher than we went.”

Placeholder quote from the OSEM President

Today, OSEM runs mentorship, scholarship, career and community programs. We work with the school administration and the PIBG (parent-teacher association) to make sure every current student feels the support of the thousands who came before them.

Our Vision

A united Semesran community that uplifts every generation.

Our Mission

  • Strengthen ties among alumni across all batches and generations.
  • Support the academic and personal growth of current SM Sains Rembau students.
  • Build a professional network that opens doors for every Semesran.
  • Serve the wider community in Rembau, Negeri Sembilan and beyond.

What We Do

Our objectives

  1. 01

    Connect

    Maintain an up-to-date alumni registry and batch networks so no Semesran loses touch.

  2. 02

    Mentor

    Pair current students with alumni for academic, university and career guidance.

  3. 03

    Fund

    Raise and manage funds for scholarships, school facilities and student welfare.

  4. 04

    Celebrate

    Organise Homecoming, reunions and sports events that keep the Semesran spirit alive.

  5. 05

    Advance

    Create professional opportunities through talks, referrals and partnerships.

  6. 06

    Serve

    Lead volunteering and outreach in Rembau and in communities where alumni live.
